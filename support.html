Support

Need help with Bitepedia? Start here — we've got answers to the most common questions.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to create an account?

Nope — Bitepedia is completely account-free. There's no sign-up, no login, and no password to forget. Everything you save, like, or add to your wishlist is stored privately on your device. If you switch phones, your data won't automatically transfer, but your wallet and your attention stay your own.

How do I upgrade to Bitepedia Pro?

Open the app and head to Settings → Upgrade to Pro. You'll see the available plans (weekly, monthly, and yearly). Payment goes through your Apple App Store or Google Play account — we never see your card details.

Pro removes all ads and gives you full access to every dish and pairing in the app.

Why am I seeing ads?

Free-tier users see ads served by Google AdMob, including occasional banner, interstitial, and native ads. There's a short ad-free grace period when you first install the app.

If you'd rather browse without ads, upgrading to Bitepedia Pro turns them off permanently for as long as your subscription is active.

How do I restore a previous Pro subscription?

If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, your subscription can be restored without paying again. Go to Settings → Restore Purchases and tap the button. Make sure you're signed into the same App Store or Google Play account you used to subscribe originally.

If Restore Purchases doesn't work after a minute or two, contact us and we'll help sort it out.

How do I cancel my Pro subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through your app store, not through Bitepedia directly. To cancel:

  • iOS: Settings app → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Bitepedia → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play app → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bitepedia → Cancel.

Your Pro access continues until the end of the current billing period. We don't offer refunds for partial periods, as this is handled by Apple and Google's policies.

Is the food content verified and culturally accurate?

We care a lot about this. Bitepedia's content is curated with diaspora communities in mind, and each dish includes a note on the cultural context behind its pairings — not just what it is, but why it's made that way.

That said, food traditions vary by region, family, and generation, and we're always learning. If something looks off, please tell us.

How do I report an incorrect dish or pairing?

We genuinely want to know. Email us at jabbrixstudio@gmail.com with the dish name, what you think is wrong, and (if you have one) a source we can check. We review every report and update the content when corrections are warranted.

How do I enable or manage push notifications?

Bitepedia uses push notifications (via OneSignal) for things like the daily trivia reminder. You can enable or disable them from your phone's system settings — look for Bitepedia under your device's Notifications settings. You can also adjust preferences inside the app under Settings → Notifications.

If your question isn't covered above, we're happy to help directly.
